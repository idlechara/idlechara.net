Meaning of my handle

I think that maybe it's time to explain why my handle is like that.

My handle is actually composed by two words. Kuky and Nekoi. The first one comes from kynku, my original nickname on internet, which was an abreviation of Kurayaminokumo, which is a song from Final Fantasy VII. It is one of my favourites songs at technical level, so I wanted to be a part of me. It can be translated as "A Darkness Cloud".

The other part comes from neko, which is how my first girlfriend tend to called me. The story is pretty much boring to be honest, as for some reason, after she started calling me like that, everyone on the class started calling me like that. So, whenever I had to use some sort of tool, my username was that in order to know the rest that it was me. Before I knew it, all my usernames had neko on them, which also suites me since I'm pretty fond of cats.

Still, there is a reason on merging both names. Basically, the one who was called neko at university it was the version of me that is open to more people, eager to be there for others. Whilst the one being kynku it was my true nature, always evaluating, judging, commanding whatever the first one has to do in order to stay within reasonable limits.

Then, KuKy_NeKoi as a handle appeared. Time passed but I do have some fondness to that name. I've tried to get rid of it ton of times but I simply can not.

Even today, both sides coexist together, but due to life development the first had become the face of the second, allowing me to blend into a society which doesn't understand reason. I'm gonna explain that summarizing my daily life, more exactly how my day went today.
