This is my first post here, I'll talk about some random things

And what better than talking about some silly things about love and such.

First of all, I'm not gonna try to convince you that my point of view is the correct one (since this topic is pretty much personal) nor that your point of view is wrong. I'll try to summarize some things that currently go through my head when I come across this concept.

What is love?

Probably one of the best definitions is this Haddaway song, because is pretty much like giving to something/someone a full loaded gun, pointing directly at your heart and you begging for the trigger not being pulled at anytime.

SPOILER: Always happens.

It's probably one of the most strange things to me, since it lacks of logical arguments and also deviates things (as most feelings do) turning people into visceal beasts rather than logical elements in a society. And this could be applied to many things actually, that's why I used the word something/someone before.

Human relationships

This is the most basic manifestation, and probably the one most harmful most of the time. Humans have use of reason, but also they develop a gregarius behaivor over time when exposed to other people. Mostly because our biology tell us to not be alone in the world and that we need more people to reach happiness (thanks hormones).

It also enables us to create connections with other people and when those connections become more deep over time, you start developing love towards your friends. Mutual trust more specifically. Not only sharing things like time, but also you start to open your heart and your inner self. But also arises opportunities for betrayal.

When such things happens, our mind tells us that something is wrong, and starts to stimulate similar brain regions as physical pain, also affecting how we see risky desicions. In some people, those brain regions are always stimulated, so it's nearly impossible to make friends and such, because you feel inmense pain which is mostly translated as depression.

And that's the case of frienship, I believe that there is no need to explain other kind of human relationships.

Hobbies

This is the one I consider is the less harmful of all. Same thing as before, but in this case it can be translated as two different signals, namely frustration or euphoria. Let's consider the case of inventors.

Most inverntors give their life to their creations, are pretty much like their children. I remember that back in the 2011 when a roommate introduced me to Arduino, and I managed to blink a led I was happy as fuck.

The sensation was simply awesome, I knew that more people managed to do the same thing as me, but for me, it was something totally new, I felt like discovering America, discovering a new world. Then I met another guy who introduced me to the robotics contests (and later it became a friend of mine) and the train didn't stopped then.

In those cases, that feeling was translated as euphoria, which enables you to continue pushing your limits. There is a picture uploaded on the ICPCNews twitter which reflects that state in a picture:

I really love that picture, and states clearly what happens when you do things right.

On the other side, you have frustration feelings. When things that you love doesn't start to work as you expect, your very same expectations render you depressed. This can become pretty much a vicious circle. But again, frustration at least can be used to fuel motivation towards the pursue of new euphoric sensations of discovery, so it all depends on what you're actually looking forward.

There are some things that I can't really stand tho. There are some people that says if you don't do things, then those things doesn't matter to you. Sometimes they're right, but most of the time, it's that you're trapped inside that vicious cicle and trying to escape.

Still, there are some things that I enjoy

There was once a girl that said to me that I was too sweet. But I prefer to see it more like I like manifesting my feelings as clear as I can. But I also enjoy being with others. My only problem is that I'm actually afraid of making connections only to break them later. This prevents me from moving forward. I know that it is a problem, but I can't do anything about it.

A case was of a certain someone that I encountered when I came to this city (the one on where I'm currently living). It was pretty much like a fated encounter for me, because for some coincidences I managed to get reunited with someone I cared the most many years ago. And then I started looking forward to step up things and to be as honest as I could. But things didn't managed to work as intended to, there was some mutual differences and points of view which managed to destroy everything. My feelings haven't changed, despite I know that she doesn't actually wants to know anthing about it. We still chat about silly things sometimes, but outside of the screen relationships are clearly out of the question.

Having said that, don't try to push people of to convince them that they can do things again

As you don't know what other experiences are, you cannot be full of yourself to think that hey, you can always try again. That applies to hobbies or things like that, but with visceral things like human relationships it doesn't work in that way. Many people tend to mix concepts of love and happiness, and to be honest, you can have one without the another or even live without any of those two. Yeah, you can always (try) to fall in love with something/someone, but that doesn't mean it that you actually want to do it.

For me, to be close to people hurts me everytime. There are contexts on whom I try to stop my irrational side in order to not affect my daily life (like in the workplace), showing a different behaivor in order to not mock other people. That's different from the me outside of that space, which is kinda pushy on most things, likes to laugh about everything, sings alone and such. I made my decision about not feeling things for people anymore and that's not gonna change anytime soon.

It's hard for me, since I can't help but relate or wanting to try to get closer to some people. Also, that doesn't prevent me from liking other people. But I don't want to repeat the same things again so I force myself to establish boundaries. It's kinda pinful sometimes but that pain is nothing compared to that gun that close people point at you.
