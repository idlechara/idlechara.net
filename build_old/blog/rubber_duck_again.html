Rubber-duck.. again.

There is something that has been bothering me in the last weeks, mostly relates on how I'm actually performing at social situations. There are some things that I think that must be the cause of them so I'll begin to list them in order to not forget them later.

Viscerality

I think that I've actually mentioned this point before, but I've noticed few things that make me lose my composture even if it is on a random talk.

Talking about academia or science philosohy

My weak point. Not gonna dive into the details but I'm not quite fond of academia (despite having given it most of my student life) and also, since I consider science that it must to be a love-driven-impulse and not a commercial thing (which let's be honest, it's okay, you have to live by doing something), my ideals make me lose my composture when talking about them.

Interpersonal relationships

Would you consider someone that you met in less than a year a friend?

I really hate how people distorted that word. True, you could have a feeling for someone to be a good match for you and then later becoming a friend. LATER.

One of the most interesting human emotions is pain, and pain is what makes friends after all. You only know whose is at your side in painful moments. Because anyone can share with you happy ones, but no one will be stay at your side when you need them the most and they won't have anything to gain by staying at your side.

Because that's how people is.

Same applies for relationships. You can't expect to someone that you just met becomes something special or important to you in a short timespan. You know: Easy come, easy go.

Jokes

The manual says that people tend to say jokes very often and it doesn't mean necesseary that it's an offense (even if you're the target of them). Either way, I don't actually understand them, so, I laught anyway. It has become rather easy for me to imitate such things, the problem is, is now that I'm doing subconciously. I hate losing my self-control.

Feelings

Lately I think that I'm actually better from depression. Despite how many people sees it, this is not something that you overcome it, it's something that's always there with you and never goes away. For the same reason there are some things that bug me.

When people say that I can try to fall in love again

It seems that for many people it's important to fall in love. For me it's just a complement. Being honest, I do wanted to after breaking with my former girlfirend, since it's something rather unkown to me. You're probably wondering Why it's unknown if you had a girlfriend before?. The answer is that I limited every feeling that I could have because I knew from the start that it a great risk to enage in something.

Risks?

Yeah, you're dating a girl that lives near your house, studies the same thing as you, is involved on the same projects as you and taking the same courses and sharing the same circle of friends. What could actually go wrong?

Because of that is that why I'm against couples in the workplace.

But to be honest, I'm not interested anymore. I mean sharing your world with someone is a rather complex topic for me (explained before), and I prefer to be alone. Also, to be with someone is dragging other person into my problems.

just because you have a good love life and you enjoy it IT DOESNT FUCKIN MEAN THAT YOU SHOULD SHOVE IT TO EVERY PERSON THAT YOU MET ON THE STREET.

Having doubts on what I'm

There are some people that say: "You're not a machine, so stop behaving like that!" But that makes me wonder, if I'm really a person. Even more, if I'm really a funcional member of the society.

I wonder it because we all know what happens to disfunctional members.

Inmaturity?

People suffer.

All people do.

Just because you learned to manage some situations that happened in your context, it doesn't give you the right to call yourself a mature person.

If you were mature enough to understand that the people is different even at celular level, then you could at least understand that people see and feel the world in a different thing. There is no such inmature thing as trying to convince another person that you're mature.

You don't know other's issues.

You don't know other people experiences.

And by giving that attitude, it is less likely that they would be sincere to you.

Trust in other people

I've discovered not too long ago that I can't trust people. But what about friends? Well, they know that I can't trust them either, since they consider me as their friend and I consider them to be too, I need to be as rational as I can in order to give them my best.

Trying to put faith in others is just silly.

Everything that you say will be used against you.

Being honest with other people

I tried to being honest with them.

They thought that I was joking. Maybe not, but at least, jugding by their reaction, it's like they actually joked with it.

Example. Last time I just splurted a random comment about that I usually don't eat a weekends. Honest comment. Which has a ton of logic, since I sleep most of the time and I'm too tired to even eat. And some people said that I should eat, that it's not bad, and started joking around the topic.

Not gonna try again.

Isolation

Probably, my isolation from people has also helped to clear my mind. So I came to the realization that I'm not actually meant to live in society. I should probably devote to my hobbies and to my 2D world.

2D will never betray you as people do.

Suicidal thoughts

Those have been worsened in the past few months. I mean, those have been with me since I have, dunno… 5 years old? I stil remember when I tried to hang myself from a tree and my cousin went "to the rescue" before I choked. Still, I've been able to manage them quite well since 14.

But they are still with me. Probably the only reason that stop me from doing so is some basic biology and human physiology knowledge, which warns me that there is no "painless death".

Still, I think that suffering from it it would be better than to wake up everyday with an urge to cut my throat every time that I go past the kitchen.

Understanding life placebos

There are ton of placebos, which make you feel happy, or at last feel like to. Having a partner is one of those, but it's because affects also your chemical composition (hormones). Not gonna deny, it's one of the best pacebos, but it's only that.

Next one is money, which allows you to supress the urge of necessity. Probably every reader should be aware of it.

Calm is one of the placebos that works the best if you have a strong mind (not my case). You know, remember to keep cam in the most dire of the situations.

Work is one of the best placebos for me. It's on where I can do the only thing that I know to do. Write code.

There are others like conversating with people that shares your interests ans such, but lately my everyday life has been limited to going to my job and then returning to my home as soon as I can and stay caged there.

Final thoughts

I hope that I don't jump from the top a building at this rate. Still, I feel better after shouting this.
